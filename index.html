itsim

Free, hands-on simulators for practicing real IT and managed-services work in your browser. Build an environment, run it, break it, fix it. Every element explains itself, and guided scenarios check your work as you go.

ITIT Lab Simulator

Build and run a company's infrastructure in a VMware vSphere-style or Microsoft Hyper-V-style console.

  • Hosts, clusters, HA, shared storage, live migration
  • VLANs, firewall rules, DHCP, DNS, site-to-site VPN
  • Active Directory, Group Policy, Entra ID sync
  • MFA, EDR, SIEM, backups, security posture score
  • 6 build scenarios with live checklists
Launch IT Lab

RMMRMM Lab Simulator

Practice an MSP technician's day in a console modeled on NinjaOne-style remote monitoring & management.

  • Clients, locations, agent policies with inheritance
  • Alert conditions with self-healing automations
  • Patch Tuesday, approvals, third-party patching
  • Ticketing with SLAs, remote sessions, backups
  • 6 practice scenarios, plus an AI tutor on claude.ai
Launch RMM LabOpen with AI assistant
No sign-up, no install, and no real systems are touched. Your lab is saved in your own browser. Works on desktop and phone; tap the ? icons for definitions, how-tos and alternative technologies.

The RMM Lab Simulator is an independent training tool modeled on NinjaOne-style workflows and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NinjaOne. Its AI assistant runs only when the simulator is opened on claude.ai.
